Privacy notice for the entry & exit system

In short: we use your phone to record when you enter and leave the school and to check, according to your timetable, whether you may leave. We do not store your location: your phone only tells us “yes, I am at school”, “no” or “unknown”. We will never ask for your email password.

Who is responsible?

AESA Prep Academy, Carrer de Montevideo, 31, 08034 Barcelona. Privacy contact: [contact email].

What do we use your data for?

  • To record entries to and exits from the school.
  • To check, according to your timetable and any permissions from the school office, whether you may leave at that moment.
  • To alert the school office about unauthorised exits, late arrivals or absences, especially for students under 18.

What data do we process?

  • First name, surname(s), class, school email and, if the school has it, your photo.
  • Your timetable and any one-off permissions created by the school office.
  • Each check-in: date and time, door, whether it was an entry or an exit, whether it was allowed and why.
  • Whether the phone was on the school premises: only “YES”, “NO” or “UNKNOWN”. The distance is worked out on your own phone; the coordinates never leave the phone and are never stored. You can only check in at school, so you need to allow location access.
  • To recognise your phone we store a random key on it (in the browser storage). Our sheet only keeps an encrypted version (hash) of that key, the date it was linked and the browser type.
  • The 6-digit code we email you is stored only in encrypted form and expires after 10 minutes.

Who else sees this data?

Only authorised school office staff. The data is kept in the school’s Google account (Google acts as data processor). The page you open when you scan is hosted on GitHub Pages and receives none of your personal data. Data is not shared with third parties unless required by law.

How long is it kept?

[To be completed by the school, e.g.: during the school year and for up to one year afterwards, unless the law requires longer.]

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection by writing to [contact email]. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the Catalan Data Protection Authority (apdcat.gencat.cat).

Unlinking your phone

On the check-in screen there is a “Not me / unlink this phone” link, which deletes the key from your phone. The school office can also unlink it.
